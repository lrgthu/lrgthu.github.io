About

Trained as a statistician and cognitive scientist, I think about how intelligence forms—and what it means for intelligence to become useful.

My work lives between models, representations, and systems. Underneath it all is a broader question: how structure becomes meaning, how meaning becomes action, and how action becomes understanding.

I’m drawn to problems that are not fully defined yet, where clarity has to be earned and judgment matters more than labels.

I build to understand. I iterate to learn. I ship to let ideas meet the world.
