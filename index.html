Research

Interpretable models for brain and behavior.

My current work studies how visual information is represented over time and across measurement modalities. I build and validate encoding-model workflows that connect neural data with image features, deep neural network layers, and behaviorally meaningful stimulus attributes.

I care about methods that are both predictive and interpretable: models should improve held-out prediction while making clear which feature spaces contribute to the explanation.
