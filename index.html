About

I work on computational approaches for understanding visual representations in the brain, with a focus on brain encoding models, structured variance partitioning, and reproducible analysis pipelines for EEG, MEG, and fMRI data.

My research connects neural data with image features, deep neural network layers, and behaviorally meaningful stimulus attributes. I care about models that are both predictive and interpretable: they should improve held-out prediction while making clear which feature spaces contribute to an explanation.
